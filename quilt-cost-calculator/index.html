Quilt Cost Calculator

Enter your materials and time. You get the total cost and a suggested selling price.

How the quilt cost calculator works

The total is materials (fabric, batting, thread) plus quilting plus your time. Longarm quilting is usually charged per square inch: about $0.015 to $0.025 for an edge-to-edge design, and more for custom work. Enter 0 if you quilt it yourself and count those hours instead.

The suggested price adds a markup on materials to cover fabric that goes unused, shipping and wear on your tools.

Questions

How much does it cost to make a queen quilt?

With about 14 yards of fabric at $13, $45 queen batting, $15 of thread and edge-to-edge longarm quilting at $0.02 per square inch, materials and quilting come to about $436 before your time.

How do quilters price their quilts?

Most add up materials and quilting, then add an hourly rate for their time. The hourly rate is what varies most, so pick one you would be happy to earn.

What does longarm quilting cost?

Edge-to-edge is often $0.015 to $0.025 per square inch. A 60" × 72" quilt is 4,320 square inches, so about $65 to $110.
