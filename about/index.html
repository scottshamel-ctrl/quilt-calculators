About

Quilt Calculators is a set of free tools for the math in quilting: backing and binding yardage, borders, block layouts and cut sizes. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

The formulas use standard quilting conventions: 1/4" seam allowances, about 40" of usable width from 42" fabric, and sizes rounded up to the nearest 1/8".
