How the on point quilt calculator works

Blocks set on point are turned 45° so they sit on their corners. Each block takes up its diagonal, which is the block size × 1.414.

The gaps along the edges are filled with side setting triangles, and the four corners with corner triangles. Side triangles are cut from a square that is the block diagonal + 1 1/4", cut twice diagonally so the long edge stays on the straight grain. Corner triangles are cut from a square of (diagonal ÷ 2) + 7/8", cut once diagonally. Sizes are rounded up to the nearest 1/8". Some quilters add a little more and trim.

Questions

How many blocks in an on point quilt?

For a layout 4 blocks across and 5 down, you need 4 × 5 + 3 × 4 = 32 blocks.

Why cut side triangles from a square cut twice?

That puts the straight grain on the long outside edge of the quilt, so the edges don't stretch.

How big is a 10 inch block on point?

Its diagonal is about 14.14", so 4 blocks across makes a center about 56 5/8" wide.
